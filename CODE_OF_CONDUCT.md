# Code of Conduct

Forlæns is committed to a welcoming, respectful and accessible open source community.

## Expected Behavior

- Be considerate, constructive and specific.
- Respect different experiences, identities and levels of expertise.
- Give and receive technical feedback in good faith.
- Make participation accessible wherever reasonably possible.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, deliberate exclusion and the publication of private information are not acceptable.

This policy applies in repositories, issues, pull requests and other project spaces. Report concerns privately to [tobias@forlaens.com](mailto:tobias@forlaens.com). Reports will be handled with discretion, and proportionate action may include editing or removing content, a warning, or temporary or permanent exclusion.
